## 2024-2025 HIGHLIGHTS

**2024:**
- Won PGA Championship at Valhalla (first major, beat Bryson DeChambeau by 1)
- Set major championship record at 21-under 263
- Won The Open Championship at Royal Troon (first to win two majors in same season since Brooks Koepka in 2018)
- First golfer to shoot final-round 65 in both majors won in same year
- Rose to career-high World No. 2 ranking
- Started working with coach Chris Como

**2025:**
- Dealt with December rib injury, struggled at The Sentry before taking recovery time
- Made cut at all four majors, T8 at The Masters
- Won Baycurrent Classic in Japan (October 2025)
- Played on U.S. Ryder Cup team (3 points in losing effort)
- Remained in World Top 3 throughout season
- Ranked outside top-125 in putting for first time, experimented with putter changes
