## KEY EQUIPMENT NOTES

**9-Wood Pioneer:**
Fleetwood was one of the first Tour players to adopt a 9-wood, stating: "You get a lot of courses where there's thick rough, firm greens and there's always a balance between having something that you can drive down the fairway but also something that you can hit into greens or out of the rough. I don't like hitting hybrids and 7-wood numbers are too close to what my 5-wood produces so we found the 9-wood to fill that gap."

**On the Mini Driver:**
"For me, if I HAD to hit a fairway, I'm more comfortable hitting a driver than a 3-wood."
