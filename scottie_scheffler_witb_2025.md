## KEY EQUIPMENT NOTES

**On the P7TW Irons:**
"What I noticed when I hit them at home was, I was able to hit different windows, so when I flighted it down, I could pitch it lower than I could the P730. When I wanted to hit it up, I could hit it higher and I saw more variability in the shots."

**On the Spider Tour X Putter:**
"I like not having to line the ball up. I line this putter up well in the middle of the face. It's very good visually. And yeah, I try not to focus too much on the results, but my process was really good this week and the results so far are pretty tough to argue with."
