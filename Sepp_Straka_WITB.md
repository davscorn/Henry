# Sepp Straka WITB Report

**Player:** Sepp Straka  
**Tour:** PGA Tour  
**Data Current As Of:** 2024/2025 Season (post-Truist Championship win)

### 1. Driver

Straka utilizes a "Max" version of the Triple Diamond head, which offers a unique combination of low spin with slightly higher MOI (forgiveness) than the standard Triple Diamond chassis.

| Manufacturer | Model | Loft | Shaft |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Callaway** | Paradym Ai Smoke Triple Diamond Max | 9° | Fujikura Ventus Blue (2024) 6 X |

### 2. Fairway Woods

Straka opts for a 7-wood over a traditional hybrid or driving iron, favoring the high launch and steep landing angle it provides. He uses distinct shaft profiles for his woods, with a "Red" (higher launch) profile in the 3-wood and "Orange" (counterbalanced) in the 7-wood.

| Club | Manufacturer | Model | Loft | Shaft |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **3-Wood** | Callaway | Paradym Ai Smoke Triple Diamond | 15° | Mitsubishi Tensei 1K Red 80 TX |
| **7-Wood** | Callaway | Paradym Ai Smoke Triple Diamond | 20° | Mitsubishi Tensei 1K Orange 80 TX |

### 3. Irons

Straka employs a "combo set" configuration, using the more forgiving cavity back ZXi5 models in his long irons and transitioning to the compact ZXi7 player's cavity for his mid-to-short irons.

| Irons | Manufacturer | Model | Shafts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **4-5** | Srixon | ZXi5 | True Temper Dynamic Gold Tour Issue X100 |
| **6-9** | Srixon | ZXi7 | True Temper Dynamic Gold Tour Issue X100 |
